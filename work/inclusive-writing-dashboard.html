Work  /  Inclusive writing dashboard

Dashboard for Inclusive Writing Platform

UX/UI Design
Overview

This dashboard is a proof of concept created for a hiring-process assignment. It's a solution for visually representing data stored in a spreadsheet, making it more readable and valuable to the platform's users. The goal was also to display correlations between variables that show meaningful impact.

Methods & tools
  • Post-its
  • Sketching
  • Prototyping
  • User testing
  • Hi-fi mockup
  • Figma
01

Data structuring

Making sense of raw data

I started by summarizing the data in the spreadsheet provided by the company and writing the values on sticky notes, dividing them into categories. This let me play around with the values and manipulate them to visualize possible relationships between them, and build graphs based on those relationships.

02

Research & inspiration

Looking outward

To gather ideas and inspiration, I looked at various existing dashboards, graphs and charts to see how they visualize different types of data and correlation. I also read through parts of The Truthful Art: Data, Charts, and Maps for Communication by Alberto Cairo.

03

Sketching

Exploring formats

I continued with rough paper sketches to try out ideas and see what worked, testing different variations of graphs suited to different purposes: composition or comparison, static or over-time.

04

Prototype

High-fidelity mockup

The result was a high-fidelity mockup containing different types of graphs: a line chart, donut charts, and horizontal segmented bar charts. Each chart reveals details on hover, as shown below.

05

Usability test

Validating the solution

To validate the idea, a brief user test was performed with two people. The goal was to make sure users could understand and perceive the visualized information without confusion. The test confirmed that people could see the correlations and draw meaningful conclusions from them.
